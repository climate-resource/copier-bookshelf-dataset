Cut the generated `renovate.json` down to the two rules that keep Renovate away from the Copier owned pins,
plus the `copier` and `pre-commit` managers.
A feedstock now inherits grouping and scheduling from `config:best-practices` rather than restating them.
This means more, smaller dependency pull requests, each arriving as soon as a release lands
rather than batched into a group and held back for three days.
It also loses the weekly Monday schedule, the `dependencies` and `template-update` labels,
and lock file maintenance.
