Escaped the free text answers where they land in a quoted file.

`dataset_name_human` went straight into `towncrier.toml`'s `title_format`,
so an answer containing a double quote produced a file that will not parse
and towncrier then failed on every changelog build.
`dataset_description` in `pyproject.toml` and `author` in `bookshelf.yaml`
had the same shape of problem.
All three are now rendered through `tojson`, which quotes and escapes them.

The `dataset_name` and `project_url` validators also carried backslash escapes
that Jinja does not understand, so answering either prompt raised a `DeprecationWarning`.
