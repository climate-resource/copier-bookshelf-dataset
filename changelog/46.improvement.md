Cuts the feedstock prompts from eight to five, of which four need a typed answer.
`dataset_name_human` now defaults to a title derived from `dataset_name`, so Enter is usually the right answer.
`project_url` and `extra_recipes` are no longer prompted, keep their defaults and are still recorded, so `copier update` cannot drop a hand-set value.
The Bookshelf SDK pin now follows the template and is no longer answerable per feedstock, so `copier update` is what moves it.
